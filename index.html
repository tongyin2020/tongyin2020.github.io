InsightBridge Global Launches DirectorAI — A Third AI Model to Help Hotels Break Free from OTA Dependency InsightBridge Global 正式推出 DirectorAI——第三款AI模型，助力酒店摆脱OTA依赖，开拓独立直客渠道

InsightBridge Global LLC today announces the official launch of DirectorAI, its third AI-powered product for the global hotel market and the first model specifically engineered to address one of the hospitality industry's most persistent structural challenges: over-reliance on online travel agencies (OTAs).

As OTA commission rates continue to compress hotel margins — often reaching 15–25% per booking — DirectorAI gives hotel operators an intelligent tool to systematically redirect demand toward owned channels. The model features four adaptive pricing objectives tailored to different revenue strategies:

  • Maximize Revenue — optimizes total top-line yield
  • Maximize Profit — factors in channel costs and operational margins
  • Maximize RevPAR — balances occupancy and rate for revenue per room
  • Maximize Direct Mix — the flagship OTA-escape mode, applying direct channel pricing bias, competitive rate weighting, and aggressive bundle packaging to convert platform-driven guests into direct bookers

DirectorAI is now live and available to hotel operators worldwide.

Launch DirectorAI

InsightBridge Global LLC 今日正式宣布推出 DirectorAI——面向全球酒店市场的第三款AI产品，也是首款专门针对酒店行业最顽固结构性难题而设计的模型：对在线旅行平台（OTA）的过度依赖。

随着OTA佣金率持续压缩酒店利润空间——每笔预订佣金往往高达15%至25%——DirectorAI为酒店运营者提供了一套智能工具，系统性地将客源需求引导至自有渠道。该模型提供四种自适应定价目标，适配不同收益战略：

  • 收益最大化——优化整体营收上限

THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK 理论框架

The Home Model 家园模型

The Home Model is a covenant-based management framework that redefines organizational resilience. Unlike Wolf Culture (performance-at-all-costs) or the Permanent Startup (perpetual disruption), the Home Model builds identity fusion — the psychological state where members experience the organization's survival as personal survival. 家园模型是一个基于契约的管理框架，重新定义了组织韧性。与狼文化（不惜一切代价追求绩效）或永续创业（持续颠覆）不同，家园模型构建身份融合——成员将组织的存亡视为个人存亡的心理状态。

Management Debt 管理负债
